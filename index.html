How to use this tool

Small Group Calculator

  1. Select the employer's county — county-specific carrier availability and rates load automatically.
  2. Add employees with their age and coverage tier. Use Add multiple ages to paste a comma-separated list.
  3. Click a tier card (Bronze / Silver / Gold) to select it. The carrier table updates instantly.
  4. Lowest-cost carrier is highlighted in green. Use Print / PDF for a client-ready summary.
  5. Rates are benchmarked to the county Silver average — obtain certified small group rate sheets from each carrier's broker portal before binding.

Individual / ICHRA Calculator

  1. Select the county — the benchmark Silver rate auto-fills and drives all affordability calculations.
  2. Enter the monthly ICHRA allowance and any dependent add-on.
  3. Add employees with age, coverage, annual income, and household size.
  4. Key rule: If the ICHRA is "affordable" (EE share ≤ 9.02% of income), the employee cannot claim ACA premium tax credits — even if their income qualifies them (100–400% FPL).
  5. The minimum allowance needed shows the exact dollar to meet the safe harbor for each employee.

Updating rates each January

  1. Go to the Edit Rates tab.
  2. Visit plainhealthplan.com/state/wisconsin — the county table shows Silver avg, Bronze avg, Gold avg, and benchmark Silver for every county.
  3. Update the benchmark Silver (age 27) column for each county. All ICHRA calculations update instantly.
  4. Update the ICHRA threshold if IRS publishes a new percentage for the new plan year.
  5. For small group rates, contact each carrier's WI broker portal for certified rate sheets.

Key compliance notes

  • Small group rates in this tool are individual market county averages used as directional benchmarks. Always obtain certified small group rate sheets before binding coverage.
  • ICHRA affordability uses the W-2 wages safe harbor. Rate-of-pay and FPL safe harbors may also apply — consult a benefits attorney.
  • This tool is for internal quoting use only and does not constitute a binding quote or insurance advice.
